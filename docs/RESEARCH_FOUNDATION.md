## Evidence grading plan

For labelled heart-failure context, also retain Pitt et al., RALES, NEJM 1999, [PMID 10471456](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10471456/). The primary paper was identified in this phase; detailed appraisal and extraction of trial-specific safety denominators remain pending. Do not merge its trial event rates with spontaneous-report frequencies.

The PRD's A/B/C badges will be project summaries, not a claimed formal GRADE assessment. Before assigning a badge, record the question/population, study designs, risk of bias, consistency, precision, directness, clinically relevant endpoints, harms, guideline recommendation and reviewer rationale. Store result direction separately (`benefit`, `no-clear-benefit`, `mixed`, `harm`, or `not-assessed`): high-quality evidence can show no benefit. All grades remain `null` until this appraisal is completed in Phase 3.
