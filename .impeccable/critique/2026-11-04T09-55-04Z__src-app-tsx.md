- Reporting charts keep source, date, denominator and limitations nearby. India and organ-mapping gaps remain explicit.

## Priority issues

### 1. [P1] The one-minute story arrives too late

**Where:** “Read the story” begins with the mechanism; “Five things to take with you” sits after extensive evidence and reporting exploration.

**Why it matters:** A pharmacy peer or evaluator has to assemble the thesis before seeing the project's strongest synthesis.

**Fix:** After the preserved opening composition, add a short three-point reading introduction with links to supporting evidence. Make “Read the story” land there; retain the fuller takeaways later. Use existing sourced findings and caveats.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`.

### 2. [P2] Research and interactions are absent from desktop wayfinding

**Where:** Desktop navigation has five links; the mobile Chapters menu includes seven, adding Research activity and Interactions.

**Why it matters:** Returning readers cannot jump directly to two substantial destinations from the desktop header.

**Fix:** Preserve the mockup's five header links and provide a compact full chapter index below the opening, or a comparable chapter disclosure. Keep chapter access consistent across devices.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.

### 3. [P2] Mobile return-to-top clips the introductory eyebrow

**Where:** At 360 px after the wordmark links to `#top`, the eyebrow occupies y66–82.5 while the sticky header covers y0–76; observed scroll position is 52.

**Why it matters:** Returning to the introduction hides part of the exact composition the user selected.

**Fix:** Correct the `#top` anchor offset to clear the full mobile header. Preserve the eyebrow and the approved composition.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`.

### 4. [P2] Evidence qualifiers are less prominent than the grade

**Where:** Small direction labels such as “Mixed Primary No Clear Benefit” and “No Clear Comparative Breast Growth Difference” sit beside stronger grade/topic elements.

**Why it matters:** These qualifications change how a reader should interpret the appraisal, yet they are harder to scan.

**Fix:** Present the existing qualification wording in sentence case at a readable body size directly beside or below the grade. Keep its factual meaning and endpoint-specific scope intact.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

**Moderate cognitive load:** chunking and choice hierarchy need work. The opening's two actions and four statistics are manageable; grouping, hierarchy and progressive disclosure are generally effective. Ten equally weighted appraisal choices and seven chapter links when the menu is open need clearer grouping. Longer select lists are hidden until opened and should not be described as simultaneously visible choices.

The opening feels confident and carefully prepared. The extended middle creates a valley through repeated chart structures and qualifications. Missingness as a finding is a strong intellectual payoff that deserves earlier framing. Methods and source access provide reassurance at the end, although the long reference list makes the finish feel archival.
