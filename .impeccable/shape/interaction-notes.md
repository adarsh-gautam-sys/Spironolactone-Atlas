# Shape — interaction and motion notes for review

Working material for the final shape brief, not a direction contract or implementation approval. The chosen visual direction will determine the exact composition.

## Reading journey

- Lead with one understandable observation and its limitation, then invite the 60-second story or direct data exploration.
- Keep product context and the mechanism explanation available early, followed by product-specific uses/evidence, reported experience, research activity, interactions and methods. Group the PRD's detailed sections into a small, named chapter system rather than fifteen competing top-level destinations.
- Every numeric claim keeps its source/date and denominator beside the visual. Underlying tables and dataset downloads remain reachable without completing an animation or a scroll sequence.
- Display project evidence grade, endpoint direction, population and review scope together. The absence of an Indian source or SOC mapping must never appear as an approval decision or a fabricated graphic.

## Motion grammar

- One signature explanatory transition per chapter at most. Use short, coordinated entrances and data transitions rather than independent perpetual effects.
- Evidence-table direction: move a reading marker through paired observation/meaning/limit rows; keep essential copy visible and correctly ordered throughout.
- Projection direction: reveal the chart's structure and annotation in reading order; transition filters with stable axes, labels and group denominators.
- Proportion direction: reveal measured known/unknown areas without changing their actual ratio; the label explains what that quantity means before the next transition.
- Familiar editorial direction: use simple section progression and restrained chart entrances; keep the established reading sequence obvious.
- Motion uses `motion/react`. Native anchor navigation remains usable. Lenis is an optional enhancement only if it preserves keyboard, focus, browser history and reduced-motion behavior.

## React Bits budget

Proposed common baseline, subject to the chosen direction:

1. **CountUp** for one introductory cohort value; keep the final value available to assistive technology and the static fallback. Avoid counting every number.
2. **BlurText** for a short non-factual headline only; no blurred methodology, source labels, medical caveats or long paragraphs.

Conditional candidates, not commitments:

- **Dock** only if a labelled floating chapter navigator fits the selected composition. A conventional text chapter rail may be better for the table direction. Magnification must not shift critical controls or reduce keyboard usability.
- **SpotlightCard** only if evidence-topic tiles survive the chosen layout; use it as a focus/selection aid in that world's grammar, not as a new bento visual system.

The ceiling is six components, not a target. Aurora, Particles, SplitText, ShinyText, ScrollReveal, MagicBento, Stack and ScrollVelocity are not selected merely because install commands exist. A decorative effect must not look like a clinical signal or measured data. Review registry dependencies and Motion-version compatibility before adding anything; install no components during shape.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

- Desktop evidence comparisons can use aligned columns; narrow screens pair each observation with its interpretation and limit in reading order, rather than shrinking a three-column table until it is unreadable.
- Put the chapter system within reach on mobile without covering charts or footnotes. Source disclosures and evidence drawers need visible controls, keyboard support, focus management and touch access.
- Under reduced motion, display final values, complete charts and all text immediately. Replace parallax, animated blur and decorative physics with static presentation. Avoid scroll hijacking, flashing and infinite ambient animation.
- Data-loading, failed-loading and genuinely absent-source states must be distinguishable. Do not replace missing values or failed requests with zero. Keep a useful text/table route through the content.

## Current boundary

The website remains the setup placeholder. Comps test hierarchy and composition; exact chart geometry, complete responsive states, semantics and data binding belong to implementation after the design is approved. No `DESIGN.md`, token values, UI components or code-level direction contract is written by shape.
