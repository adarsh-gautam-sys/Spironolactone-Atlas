# Shape direction derivation — working notes

Confirmed product: a 60-second evidence-led explanation for pharmacy peers and evaluators, followed by deeper source/data exploration. Web; Read mode. Comp-first was explicitly chosen by the user. These are candidates, not a direction contract or implementation approval.

Unique mechanism: product-specific label scope, reproducible report counts and endpoint-specific evidence are read together, with uncertainty visible where the sources cannot establish a claim.

Scene and cultural home: a pharmacy student sharing a research project with classmates and an evaluator after a presentation; familiar with clinical teaching, journal clubs, research posters, lab instruments and source-annotated figure plates.

Binding input: the PRD's clinical/editorial mood, deep ink ground, off-white text, coral primary emphasis and teal therapeutic/evidence context; amber/red reserved for risk. The placeholder is toolchain proof, not incumbent visual authority.

The category rut is a glowing molecule above a dashboard of identical statistic cards. Its predictable opposite is an unstyled white journal page. Neither is a candidate. The literal atlas metaphor occupies only candidate four.

## Seven grounded candidates, ranked before the roll

1. **Grand Rounds Projection** — a dark medical-teaching projection with one large explanatory evidence figure, precise annotations and a chapter spine; leads understanding before opening the data.
2. **Conference Proceedings Folio** — a contemporary pharmaceutical research proceedings spread: a strong abstract, oversized figure references and dense, well-captioned evidence plates; makes provenance feel native to reading.
3. **Instrument Trace Record** — the grammar of analytical laboratory traces, calibrated axes and recorded observations; the real report-year trace becomes the organizing continuous figure, never a fictional assay.
4. **Teaching Atlas Plates** — numbered teaching plates with editorial captions, generous serif headings and cross-reference marks; a single literal atlas direction, without inventing anatomical evidence or SOC maps.
5. **Pharmaceutical Exhibition Graphics** — the large-scale typography and navigable wall panels of a science exhibition/conference; one strong typographic thesis followed by readable evidence stations.
6. **Journal Club Evidence Table** — a study-comparison table expanded into a spatial reading surface, with question, endpoint, direction and source occupying stable positions; scrutiny becomes an interaction rather than a sidebar.
7. **Teaching Museum Collection** — a curated educational collection with numbered exhibits and visible provenance, where an overview progressively resolves into individual evidence objects; no fictitious physical samples or clinical proof photographs.

These span teaching environments, publications, instruments/data notation and exhibition/collection systems. Motion and React Bits selections will serve the chosen structure, with static and reduced-motion alternatives and a six-component ceiling.
